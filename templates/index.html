About {{ site.name }}

A community built around movement

Welcome to JAM Dance Academy, where we celebrate your passion for dance. As a vibrant Bollywood dance community in Belgium, we create an inclusive space for all enthusiasts to express themselves through movement.

Our mission is to spread the joy of dance and movement — we're here to inspire and guide you on your dance journey, whether you're a beginner or looking to refine your skills. Join us to experience the joy of dance and embrace the rhythm of life at JAM.
